### Software Crisis — Discussion (Junior vs Senior)

**Junior:** What is the software crisis?  
**Senior:** It refers to the situation where developing software became very difficult due to increasing demand and complexity, while methods, tools, and skills did not improve at the same pace.

**Junior:** What problems did it cause?  
**Senior:** Many:
- Projects exceeded budget  
- Delays in delivery  
- Poor software quality  
- Low efficiency  
- Software often failed to meet user requirements  

**Junior:** Was it only a technical issue?  
**Senior:** No, it was also a management problem. Causes included:
- Poor project management  
- Lack of proper training  
- Low-skilled team members  
- Low productivity improvements  

**Junior:** What were the clear signs of this crisis?  
**Senior:** 
- High maintenance cost  
- Projects running overtime  
- Inefficient software  
- Poor quality systems  
- Difficult to modify, debug, and enhance  
- Poor resource utilization  

**Junior:** How was it solved?  
**Senior:** There is no single fix, but software engineering was introduced as a solution. It provides a structured and disciplined approach.

**Junior:** What does software engineering improve?  
**Senior:** 
- Reduces cost and delays  
- Improves quality  
- Ensures proper delivery  
- Uses skilled resources  
- Meets user requirements  

**Junior:** Give me a short interview answer.  
**Senior:**  
Software crisis refers to the challenges faced in software development due to increasing complexity and demand, leading to delays, budget overruns, and poor-quality software. Software engineering was introduced to solve these issues through structured processes and better management.
